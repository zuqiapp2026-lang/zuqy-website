Privacy Policy

Pre-launch notice: ZUQY is currently under development. This page will be updated with the final legal entity, contact details, data-retention periods and production service providers before public launch.

Information we may process

Depending on the features used, ZUQY may process phone and OTP verification data, profile information, chat and call-related records, coin/payment records, earnings and payout information, verification/KYC information, support communications, and technical/security data.

Why we use information

We use information to provide and secure accounts, enable app features, process eligible transactions, prevent abuse, provide support, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Sensitive information

KYC and payout information is not intended to be public. Production systems are intended to use appropriate access controls and security safeguards.

Your choices

Users will be able to request account deletion and applicable data access or correction through ZUQY support and in-app controls when the service launches.
